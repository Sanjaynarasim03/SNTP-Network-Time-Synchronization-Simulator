Localhost Wireshark Capture Instructions

To capture and inspect real SNTP UDP packets generated by this simulator in Wireshark:

  1. Open Wireshark and select the Loopback (lo / lo0) interface.
  2. Set the display filter to: udp.port == 1123 or ntp.
  3. Click "Start Sync" or "Sync Once" in this dashboard.
  4. Inspect the 48-byte UDP datagram in Wireshark and verify that the fields (LI, VN=4, Mode=3/4, Stratum, Timestamps) match our Packet Inspector tab exactly!
